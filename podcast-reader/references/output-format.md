# Episode note format

Write notes to `<data>/notes/<episode-id>.md` with this shape:

```markdown
# Episode title

- Source: Show name
- Published: YYYY-MM-DD
- Duration: HH:MM:SS
- Original: https://...
- Transcript: publisher captions | platform captions | local ASR

## In one minute

Three to six sentences describing what the episode actually argues.

## Timeline

- [00:00] Opening topic — one-sentence summary
- [12:34] Chapter title — one-sentence summary

## Key ideas

1. Claim, supporting reasoning, and any important qualification. [12:34]

## Terms explained

- **Term** — plain-language explanation in this episode's context. [18:20]

## Questions worth asking

- A grounded follow-up question.
```

Use timestamps from transcript evidence, not invented chapter boundaries. Keep quotations short. Mark uncertain speaker names as `Speaker` rather than guessing.
